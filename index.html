ACS members and Community Associates among VIC users

How to read these figures

Community Associates (CAs) are not ACS members. The matched users are therefore shown in two groups: ACS members (Student, Regular, Society Affiliate and Emeritus) and Community Associates. Use the account-type switch at the top to view either group on its own.

This dashboard shows the overlap between VIC users and ACS records, matched by email address. It does not show that VIC created these members or CAs: a person counted here simply appears in both lists.

The join-timing groups describe sequence only:

  • Already in ACS before VIC: they joined ACS (as a member or CA) before signing up to VIC, so VIC could not have brought them in.
  • Joined ACS after VIC (within 30 days / 30+ days): they joined ACS after signing up to VIC. The order is known, but the cause is not. They may have joined through VIC, or independently through other ACS channels, an institution, an event or a colleague.

The actual effect of VIC on ACS membership would need a comparison group or conversion tracking, which this data does not include. Matching is by email, so people who used different emails on VIC and ACS are not matched; the overlap is therefore a minimum, not a full count.

Filters

Matched users (members and CAs)

Name-only look-alikes are excluded. Gender, age and degree are self-reported on few records, so most matched users fall in the “not provided” boxes. See the Excel report for emails and full detail.
